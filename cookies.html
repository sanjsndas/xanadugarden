    What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information and help improve the user experience.

    Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them, while session cookies are deleted as soon as you close your browser.

    How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Essential / Strictly Necessary: These cookies are required for the website to function properly (e.g., to remember your cookie preferences or to enable secure logins).
    • Performance / Analytics: We use cookies to collect aggregated data about how visitors use our site — which pages are visited most often, and if they encounter errors. This helps us improve our content and design.
    • Functionality: These cookies remember your preferences (such as language or region) to enhance your experience.
    • Third-Party: We may use trusted third-party services (e.g., Google Analytics) that set their own cookies to help us understand site usage. These cookies are governed by the respective third party's privacy policy.

    Your Cookie Choices

    When you first visit our site, you will see a banner asking for your consent to set cookies. You can:

    You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, if you disable cookies, some parts of our website may not function as intended.

    For more information on how to manage cookies in your browser, please visit your browser's help center or the About Cookies website.
